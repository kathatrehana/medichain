The ledger

Each block stores its index, timestamp, data, the hash of the previous block, and its own SHA-256 hash. Change anything and every block after it breaks.

Educational prototype. The blockchain is simulated in the browser (localStorage) on a single node. Hyperledger Fabric with proof of authority is the proposed production design. Prices are sample values. Medicine information is general and does not replace a doctor or pharmacist.
